Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the official ZUSA website and verification service may collect, use, protect, and retain information when you use the service.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the ZUSA website, verification flow, and related services that directly link to this policy. Third-party services are governed by their own policies.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the verification flow, information may include your Roblox account identifier, public Roblox profile information, OAuth authorization information, verification status, security or anti-abuse information, and technical information such as IP address, browser information, timestamps, and request logs. Only information reasonably necessary for the service should be processed.

3. Roblox OAuth

If you choose to verify through Roblox OAuth 2.0, you authorize the information requested by the ZUSA application within the scopes presented during authorization. ZUSA does not receive your Roblox password. OAuth credentials and tokens must be handled securely and only for the purposes for which authorization was granted.

4. How We Use Information

Information may be used to authenticate users, complete verification, associate a verified Roblox account with an authorized ZUSA service, prevent duplicate or fraudulent verification, detect abuse, maintain security, troubleshoot technical problems, and provide support.

5. Information We Do Not Intend to Collect

The service should not request your Roblox password, payment card details, or unrelated sensitive information. Never provide credentials to a page claiming to be ZUSA outside the official verification flow.

6. Third-Party Providers

The service may use Roblox OAuth, Netlify hosting, Discord, or other infrastructure and services required to operate ZUSA. Those providers may independently process information under their own terms and privacy policies. ZUSA is not responsible for privacy practices outside its control.

7. Cookies, Storage, and Logs

The website may use essential cookies, session or browser storage, security logs, and similar technical mechanisms needed to operate OAuth and protect the service. Non-essential analytics or tracking should only be introduced with appropriate disclosure.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be used to protect information. No online service can guarantee complete security, and users should protect their own accounts and never share passwords or authorization credentials.

9. Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for verification, security, operational, support, or legal purposes. Where appropriate and technically feasible, users may request correction or deletion of information associated with their verification.

10. Children's Privacy

ZUSA does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Users must also comply with the age requirements and policies of Roblox and any other third-party platform used with ZUSA.

11. International Use

Because ZUSA may rely on online infrastructure and third-party providers, information may be processed in locations where those providers operate, subject to applicable law and their respective policies.

12. Your Requests

For questions, privacy requests, or concerns about verification data, contact the ZUSA administration through its official support channels. Requests may require reasonable identity or account verification and may be limited where retention is required for security or law.

13. Changes

This policy may be updated when the service, verification process, or legal requirements change. The current version published on this page applies to future use of the service.

14. Contact

Privacy questions should be directed to the ZUSA administration through its official support channels.
